Record responsibly

Recording laws vary by jurisdiction. Some U.S. states — including California, Illinois, Washington, Pennsylvania and Florida — require everyone being recorded to consent. The EU (GDPR) and China (PIPL) impose additional duties.

You are responsible for having the right to record and process any audio you use with this tool.
